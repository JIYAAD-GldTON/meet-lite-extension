🔍 How to Test in an Actual Google Meet Call:

1
Check MeetLite's In-Call Floating HUD

During any live meeting, look at the floating HUD in the bottom-right corner. The ⬆ Up and ⬇ Down chips update live every second from the real WebRTC socket.

2
Check Chrome's Built-In WebRTC Diagnostic Tool

Open a new browser tab and type chrome://webrtc-internals/. Click on your active Meet peer connection to see Google Chrome's native bandwidth graph confirming the capped bitrate.

3
Check Google Meet's Native Troubleshooting Panel

Inside the Google Meet call, click More options (⋮) → Troubleshooting and help → Audio and video network stability. You will see Meet's native network graph running in the green safe zone.
